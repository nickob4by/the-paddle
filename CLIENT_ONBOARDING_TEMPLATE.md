# 🏓 Court Booking System - Client Onboarding Questionnaire

> **How to use**: Copy and paste the message template below directly to your new client on WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, Messenger, or Email.

---

### 📩 Message to Send to Client:

```markdown
Hi [Client Name]! 👋

We are ready to set up your online court booking, reservation, and POS system! 🏓⚡

To customize the system with your brand, please share the following details:

1. 🏷️ Business & Facility Details
   • Facility / Club Name: [e.g., Smash Point Pickleball Club]
   • Tagline / Subtitle: [e.g., Premier Indoor Pickleball Hub]
   • Address / Location: [e.g., Banilad, Cebu City]
   • Contact Phone Number: [e.g., 0917-xxx-xxxx]
   • Contact Email: [e.g., contact@yourclub.com]

2. 🎨 Logo & Branding
   • Logo file (High-resolution PNG with transparent background preferred)
   • If available, both dark background & light background versions

3. ⏰ Operating Hours
   • Opening Time: [e.g., 6:00 AM]
   • Closing Time: [e.g., 11:00 PM or 2:00 AM]

4. 🏟️ Courts & Rates
   • Number of Courts: [e.g., 2 courts]
   • Court Names: [e.g., Court 1 (Championship), Court 2 (Standard)]
   • Regular / Daytime Hourly Rate: [e.g., ₱350 / hour]
   • Evening / Peak Hourly Rate: [e.g., ₱500 / hour]
   • Evening Rate Start Time: [e.g., 5:00 PM]

5. 💳 GCash Payment Details
   • GCash Account Name: [e.g., Juan Dela Cruz / Club Name]
   • GCash Mobile Number: [e.g., 0917-xxx-xxxx]
   • GCash Merchant QR Code (Screenshot or image file)

6. 🔐 Admin Access
   • Desired 6-digit Admin PIN: [e.g., 123456]

Once you reply with these details, your booking system will be live and ready for testing! 🚀
```
